---
name: Training inquiry records
description: Product distinction between lightweight inquiry contacts and complete class requests.
---

Store preliminary training inquiries as lightweight records. One inquiry may group multiple people, each with a name and phone number, while division and location are shared by the inquiry. Do not require certification, dates, attendee counts, or email until the inquiry is converted into a full request.

**Why:** Admins need to capture calls and early interest before enough scheduling information exists to create a complete training request, and several people from the same division and location may be part of that initial contact.

**How to apply:** New inquiry features should preserve grouped people with shared division/location while keeping initial contact capture separate from the approval and scheduling workflow for full requests.
