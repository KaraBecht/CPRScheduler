---
name: Training inquiry records
description: Product distinction between lightweight inquiry contacts and complete class requests.
---

Store preliminary training inquiries as lightweight records containing contact name, phone number, division, and location. Do not require certification, dates, attendee counts, or email until the inquiry is converted into a full request.

**Why:** Admins need to capture calls and early interest before enough scheduling information exists to create a complete training request.

**How to apply:** New inquiry features should preserve the distinction between initial contact capture and the existing approval/scheduling workflow for full requests.
